**Track:** Gaming  **Division:** New Experience

## Description (197 words)
**Inspiration.** I wanted a seated game that only makes sense on your table, where your hands do natural things: lay a plank, cup something fragile, poke a curious creature.

**What it is.** Wisp Hollow is a mixed-reality tabletop game. Your table becomes a tiny valley, and autonomous wisps try to reach a fire. You never steer them: you pinch planks to bridge rivers, hold an open palm over them in storms, and pinch the air to raise mountains. Objects Quest detects on your table become mountains too. Seven levels add night gloomlings, wind and floods, plus a Daily Dusk. Rescued wisps move into a valley that grows between sessions. It's built for short seated sessions: one round takes about three minutes.

**How I built it.** Built with the Immersive Web SDK and Three.js, using raw WebXR hand joints plus plane and mesh detection. Everything is procedural, so it starts fast. It supports hands, controllers, gaze, voice and a high-contrast mode.

**Note.** The demo video was recorded in an XR emulator with a scripted demo hand, not on a headset.

**Next.** Spatial anchors, shared-table co-op, and terrain that follows your real furniture. I also want a proper soundtrack.
